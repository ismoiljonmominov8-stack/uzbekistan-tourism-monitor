Number of visits to parks in Uzbekistan reaches 27 million
12 August 2026
According to the National Statistics Committee, as of 1 January 2026, there were 161 parks of culture and recreation operating in Uzbekistan.
Their number increased by 25 compared with 2024. The total area of these parks amounted to 1.8 thousand hectares.
During 2025, 27 million visits to these parks were recorded. This was an increase of 3.2 million visits compared with 2024.
Number of visits and parks by region:
Tashkent city — 14.5 million visits (29 parks);
Namangan region — 9.2 million visits (5);
Bukhara region — 631 thousand visits (11);
Samarkand region — 537.1 thousand visits (17);
Fergana region — 384 thousand visits (8);
Andijan region — 365.5 thousand visits (20);
Jizzakh region — 356 thousand visits (4);
Navoi region — 327.5 thousand visits (9);
Surkhandarya region — 276.1 thousand visits (14);
Kashkadarya region — 186 thousand visits (5);
Republic of Karakalpakstan — 120.2 thousand visits (9);
Khorezm region — 101.1 thousand visits (14);
Syrdarya region — 34 thousand visits (13);
Tashkent region — 16.7 thousand visits (3).
