Tourist agencies provided services worth 1.9 trillion soums
02 September 2025
According to the National Statistics Committee, in January-July 2025, the volume of services provided by travel agencies, tour operators, booking services and other related services amounted to 1.9 trillion soums.
This figure increased by 43.9% compared to the corresponding period last year.
Volume of services provided by travel agencies by region:
Rep. Karakalpakstan - 4.9 billion soums
Andijan region - 8 billion soums
Bukhara region - 26.5 billion soums
Jizzakh region - 6 billion soums
Kashkadarya region - 2.8 billion soums
Navoi region - 5.3 billion soums
Namangan region - 6.5 billion soums
Samarkand region - 252.1 billion soums
Surkhandarya region - 5.6 billion soums
Syrdarya region - 0.1 billion soums
Tashkent region - 46 billion soums
Fergana region - 15.4 billion soums
Khorezm region - 62 billion soums
Tashkent city - 1,493.8 billion soums
