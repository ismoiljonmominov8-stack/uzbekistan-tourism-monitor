Which countries do Uzbeks travel to most for tourist purposes?
09 June 2025
According to the National Statistics Committee, in January-April 2025, a total of 2,116.9 thousand citizens of Uzbekistan traveled to foreign countries for tourist purposes.
This figure increased by 404.5 thousand people or 23.6% compared to the same period last year.
The number of Uzbek citizens who traveled to foreign countries in January-April of this year by country is as follows:
Kyrgyz Republic - 899 thousand people;
Tajikistan - 388.4 thousand people;
Kazakhstan - 354.9 thousand people;
Saudi Arabia - 133.6 thousand people;
Russia - 93.9 thousand people;
Turkey - 64.2 thousand people;
UAE – 58.8 thousand people;
Egypt – 22.4 thousand people;
Thailand – 15.4 thousand people;
Vietnam – 11.4 thousand people;
other countries – 74.6 thousand people.
