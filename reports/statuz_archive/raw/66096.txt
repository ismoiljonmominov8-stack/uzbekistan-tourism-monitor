From India to Italy: citizens of TOP-20 countries who came to Uzbekistan for educational purposes
05 January 2026
According to the National Statistics Committee, in January-November 2025, a total of 37.2 thousand foreign citizens visited Uzbekistan for educational purposes.
This indicator increased by 54.3% compared to the corresponding period last year.
The number of foreign citizens who came to Uzbekistan for study in 11 months of this year by country is as follows:
India - 16.3 thousand people
Turkmenistan - 8.9 thousand people
Tajikistan - 2.7 thousand people
Pakistan - 1.7 thousand people
China - 1.4 thousand people
South Korea - 870 people
Kyrgyz Republic - 823 people
Egypt - 737 people
Philippines - 501 people
USA - 464 people
Turkey - 357 people
Afghanistan - 291 people
Jordan - 194 people
Great Britain - 179 people
Russia - 164 people
South Africa - 155 people
Iran - 115 people
Kazakhstan - 113 people
Bangladesh - 97 people
Italy - 94 people
