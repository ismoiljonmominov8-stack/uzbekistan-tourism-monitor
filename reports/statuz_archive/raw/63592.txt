4.8 million people visited the museums of Uzbekistan
28 August 2025
According to the National Statistics Committee, as of January 1, 2025, the number of museums in Uzbekistan (including branches) was 138.
Museums were visited by 4.8 million people in 2024.
Of these, 1.4 million are foreign tourists.
TOP-5 museums with the largest number of visitors in our country:
Samarkand State Museum-Reserve - 1,035,325 people
Directorate of the Registan Ensemble - 908,225
State Museum of Glory - 656,355 people
Bukhara State Museum-Reserve - 489,208 people
Ichan-Kala State Museum-Reserve - 456,917 people
