Selective observation of inbound and outbound tourism in the territory of the Republic of Uzbekistan
31 May 2024
Regular selective observations on inbound and outbound tourism in the territory of the Republic of Uzbekistan since May 2024, surveys of foreign tourists have been conducted in all regions of the republic.
On the recommendation of the United Nations World Tourism Organization (UNWTO), the survey is conducted at least once every two years, starting in 2018.
In particular, on May 30, 2024, an UNWTO international expert was invited to the interview process conducted by interviewers at Tashkent Islam Karimov International Airport, and the expert shared his thoughts.
It should be noted that these observations determine the procedure for conducting selective surveillance in the field of inbound and outbound tourism to the territory of the Republic of Uzbekistan. It also provides a full accounting of the volume of tourism in the republic's exports, the formation of indicators of tourism statistics and auxiliary tourism accounting.
Based on the monitoring results, data such as the flow of tourists, the purpose and duration of the trip of tourists, the structure of expenses of foreign tourists and residents of Uzbekistan who have traveled abroad are generated.
The organization and conduct of this observation will be carried out jointly with the Ministries of Tourism and Cultural Heritage, Investment and Foreign Trade.
The survey will last until June 6 of this year, it is expected that about 14 thousand respondents (foreign tourists) will take part in the survey.
