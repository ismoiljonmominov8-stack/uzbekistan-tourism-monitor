Uzbekistan welcomed 10.3 million tourists in 9 months
16 October 2026
According to the National Statistics Committee of the Republic of Uzbekistan, a total of 10,306,786 foreign citizens visited Uzbekistan for tourism purposes in January–September 2026.
This figure increased by 1.7 million people, or 20.4%, compared with the corresponding period of the previous year.
The TOP 10 countries with the largest number of tourists visiting Uzbekistan were:
Kyrgyz Republic — 2,844,201;
Kazakhstan — 2,404,638;
Tajikistan — 2,274,288;
Russia — 983,407;
Afghanistan — 380,190;
China — 373,778;
Turkmenistan — 256,546;
Türkiye — 148,772;
India — 53,778;
Germany — 42,973.
