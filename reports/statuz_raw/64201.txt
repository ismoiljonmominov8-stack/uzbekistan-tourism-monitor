7.5 million foreign tourists visited Uzbekistan in 8 months
29 September 2025
According to the National Statistics Committee, in January–August 2025 a total of 7.5 million foreign citizens visited Uzbekistan for tourism purposes.
Compared to the same period last year, this figure increased by almost 2.5 million people, or 49.1%.
The number of visitors by country is as follows:
Kyrgyz Republic — 2,164,521 people;
Kazakhstan — 1,769,910 people;
Tajikistan — 1,699,984 people;
Russia — 663,296 people;
Afghanistan — 304,510 people;
Turkmenistan — 243,512 people;
China — 135,408 people;
Turkey — 113,109 people;
India — 42,256 people;
Republic of Korea — 30,039 people;
Other countries — 349,849 people.
The number of visitors by purpose of travel is as follows:
visiting relatives — 5,166,373 people;
travel — 1,148,469 people;
official — 837,535 people;
commercial — 287,747 people;
treatment — 62,054 people;
study — 14,216 people.
