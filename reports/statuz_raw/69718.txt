Uzbekistan welcomed 7.7 million tourists in 7 months
11 August 2026
According to the National Statistics Committee, 7,749,308 foreign nationals visited Uzbekistan for tourism purposes in January–July 2026.
This figure increased by 1.4 million people, or 22.6%, compared with the corresponding period of 2025.
Top 10 countries by number of tourists visiting Uzbekistan:
Kyrgyz Republic — 2,177,108;
Kazakhstan — 1,855,562;
Tajikistan — 1,728,978;
Russia — 710,824;
Afghanistan — 281,200;
China — 269,735;
Turkmenistan — 192,830;
Türkiye — 111,908;
India — 32,462;
Republic of Korea — 26,512.
