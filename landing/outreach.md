test fair). **Pinterest pins link to A or B directly**, because each pin already carries one message type.

## 4. Reddit — answer, don't advertise

Find recent questions in r/travel, r/solotravel, r/backpacking, r/uzbekistan ("Is Uzbekistan worth it?",
"Central Asia itinerary", "Samarkand or Bukhara?"). Answer the question first; add the link only when it helps,
and in no more than about 1 of every 10 of your posts or comments. Read each community's rules before posting.

Example answer:
> Yes — and it is easier than most people think. U.S. citizens can enter visa-free for up to 30 days (since January
> 2026), and the fast train does Tashkent → Samarkand in about 2 hours and Samarkand → Bukhara in about 1.5.
> A classic week is 2 days Tashkent, 2 Samarkand, 2 Bukhara, 1 Khiva. Book trains 30–45 days ahead on the national
> railway site. I put together a short guide with the route and costs for a university project:
> …/visit/index.html?utm_source=reddit

## 5. Pinterest (free business account)

1. Create a free Pinterest **business** account and a board named **Uzbekistan travel**.
2. Bulk upload: Pinterest → Create → **Bulk create Pins** → upload `landing/outreach/pinterest_pins.csv`
   (the 10 pin images are already online, the file points to them). If bulk upload is not offered on your
   account, create the 10 pins by hand: image from `docs/visit/pins/`, title, description and link from the CSV.
3. Post 1–2 pins a day rather than all at once.

Pins a1–a5 link to version A, b1–b5 to version B; the same five photos are used in both sets so only the message differs.

## 6. QR cards for the TITF fair (25–27 November)

Print `landing/outreach/qr_cards_A4.pdf` on thick paper (A4, 100% scale, no "fit to page") and cut along the lines:
10 cards per sheet. The QR code opens the 50/50 link labelled `qr`. Give them to foreign guests and to tour
operators — and use the conversation to ask operators to become partners (message 2).
